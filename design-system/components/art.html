Generated art

There are no image files in this project. Boards, swipe photos, trip cards and the detail hero all render <Art> from an { sky, accent, motif } object. The monospace caption names the photograph the art stands in for, and doubles as a shot list. Three aspect ratios: .art 4:3, .art--tall 3:4, .art--wide 16:9.

The stripe fill is the placeholder every motif currently falls through to. As real motifs are drawn they replace it one at a time, so the app is never broken — only progressively less abstract.
